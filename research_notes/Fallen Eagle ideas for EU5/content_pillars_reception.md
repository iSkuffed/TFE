# The Fallen Eagle (CK3): Content, Design Pillars, History and Reception

Research method note: Steam Workshop pages were read directly (main page, all 7 change-note pages, submod pages, and a collection's discussion threads). The CK3 wiki (ck3.paradoxwikis.com) returned a JavaScript bot challenge, Reddit returned HTTP 403, and Paradox Mods is JS-rendered. Anything that depends on those three sources comes only from search snippets or is listed under Gaps. TFE's own Workshop comment section could not be reached; the comments URL redirects to the description page, so comments may be disabled.

## 1. Stated design pillars and goals

### Takeaway
The team pitches TFE as a *faithful, actively researched* recreation of the transition from Classical Antiquity to the Middle Ages across Europe, Africa and Asia. That authenticity has three layers: map and politics, cultures and religions, and bespoke governments. On top of it sits curated historical-figure content and a full audiovisual identity (Late Roman UI, original soundtrack, new 3D assets). Their marketing hook is "save Rome, or watch it fall and reclaim it later".

### Cited Findings
- Official one-line pitch: "a historical total conversion mod that brings the tumultuous and transformative world of Late Antiquity to Crusader Kings III. The mod seeks to faithfully recreate the historical, cultural, and religious developments that occurred as Europe, Africa, and Asia slowly transitioned from Classical Antiquity and into the Medieval Era." — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- "The World of Late Antiquity" pillar: "a detailed and actively-researched historical, political, and cultural layout of Europe, Africa, and Asia during Late Antiquity ... filled with historical cultures and religions, new governments, new activities and decisions, and other forms of flavor that all work in tandem to breathe life into this historical period." — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- The "Unique Historical Figures" pillar lists people you can play or meet: Iulianus 'the Apostate', Flavius Stilicho, Constantinus III, Alaric, Odoacer, Iulius Nepos, Theodoric 'the Amal', Flavius Belisarius, Theodora and Khosrau 'Anushirvan'. — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- Every start date is "filled with unique content that reflect Late Antiquity's tumultuous nature". The examples given are Alaric's movements ending in the Sack of Rome (410) and Iustinianus's campaigns in North Africa and Italia (533–554). — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- Official social-media framing: "In The Fallen Eagle, will you save Western Rome? Or will you watch it collapse from the safety of the east, ready to be reclaimed again at a later date?" — [The Fallen Eagle on X](https://x.com/FallenEagleCK3/status/1554061485072056321)
- Scope, per wiki and search snippets: "the Early Medieval Era (361 – 867)", with 632 and 768 planned as further start dates. — [CK3 Wiki (snippet)](https://ck3.paradoxwikis.com/The_Fallen_Eagle)
- The current flagship update, "Imperial College", "reworks the governments, mechanics, and flavor of the Roman World" and targets CK3 1.19 with the All Under Heaven DLC. Owning Roads to Power and All Under Heaven is "highly recommended". — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- The mod leans heavily on game rules so players can tune historicity. Examples: splitting the "larp" rule into Larp / Fictional Larp / Dead Cultures, a game rule for migration strength, a toggle for military office events, and a rule to switch off "imperial competence" "for more casual games". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3); [Change notes p5](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=5)

### Inferences
- The pillars are (1) a researched map, cultures and religions, (2) Rome-specific systems such as the imperial government, collapse, legions and dioceses, (3) named characters with scripted opening dilemmas, (4) a full aesthetic identity, and (5) player-adjustable historicity through game rules. That lines up well with the user's "fun over accuracy" preference: TFE puts accuracy in the setup and fun in the alt-history decisions.
- "Save Rome or reclaim it later" is the emotional core. The collapse is meant to be avoidable but threatening, and restoration is a long-term goal for everyone, not only the Romans.

### Gaps
- I could not find a long-form developer manifesto, interview, or Paradox "mod spotlight" article. Searches for PC Gamer, RPS and TheGamer coverage returned nothing.
- I could not see the Imperial College change log. The recent Steam change notes (Mar 2024 to Sep 2026) have empty bodies, and the detailed notes appear to be on Discord or Reddit, which were inaccessible.

## 2. Bookmarks, start dates and featured characters

### Takeaway
There are four official starts: 361, 395, 476 and 532. 632 is announced with no date, and 768 appears in older planning. 395 is the start YouTubers most often use for "save Rome" and AI timelapse videos. A popular submod adds 409 and 457.

### Cited Findings
- Official start dates are 361, 395, 476 and 532, with "632 AD's release remaining TBD". — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- Order the starts were added, reconstructed from change notes:
  - 395 was the original era at launch (Sep 29, 2020). 395 content is referenced in later notes, e.g. "Fixed initial 395 Gupta war" and "Honorius bookmark". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3); [p7](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=7)
  - 476 was added on Oct 1, 2022 ("476 added!"). — [Change notes p4](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=4)
  - A "House of Sassan" bookmark came with the House of Sassan update (Jan 15, 2023). — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
  - 361 arrived with "Reign of the Apostate" (Mar 31, 2023). Its Paradox Mods listing is titled "The Fallen Eagle - 'The Reign of the Apostate'". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3); [Paradox Mods](https://mods.paradoxplaza.com/mods/23460/Any)
  - 532: the patch notes reference a "bm_523_reconquest" localisation file (Justinian's Reconquest). — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- In the 1.9 patch the team made the bookmark menu's year tabs "more compact to make space for the future planned bookmarks". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- 476 is built around Iulius Nepos. He gets an opening event chain (invade Italy or stay in Illyria), then a decision to prepare an invasion and ask the Eastern Roman Empire (ERE) for help. If he wins, he chooses independence, alliance or tributary status. Hooks and prestige vary with how much the ERE helped. Nepos also starts with a pressed claim on the WRE and on Italy. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- Submod "More Startdates and Bookmarks for Fallen Eagle" (LanLithos) adds 409 and 457. It recommends emperors Honorius, Theodosius II, Majorian and Leo I, and usurpers Constantinus, Maximus, Priscus Attalus, Jovinus, Heraclianus and Ricimer. It has 190 ratings and was updated Mar 2025 for TFE 25.6.12 / CK3 1.16. Comments complain about messy 457 de jure borders in France and missing education traits. The author says there is "No plan about earlier start dates yet". — [Workshop 2740278172](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2740278172)
- YouTube content clusters on 395 and the West: "Can Stilicho Save Rome?", "CK3: The Fallen Eagle – A.I Only Timelapse (395 – 1444)", "CK 3 Timelapse The Fallen Eagle-395", "Restoring The Western ROMAN EMPIRE In Ck 3 Fallen Eagle Was INCREDIBLE", "The Last Roman". There is also a Belisarius campaign playlist for the 532 start. — [YouTube: Stilicho](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bcwC7aIJUsE); [YouTube: AI timelapse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jpphcabbk-0); [YouTube: 395 timelapse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMVlEk-sbAE); [YouTube: Restoring WRE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YHRzpdyLG7A); [YouTube: Belisarius playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLM5F34q8Dla_i2E6ULf-eANw3nxU8qOph)

### Inferences
- The heroes of 395 are Stilicho, Honorius, Alaric and Arcadius. The EU5 mod's 395 start therefore covers TFE's most-played bookmark. Stilicho-style "a general holds a failing empire together" is the fantasy creators keep choosing.
- TFE's strongest pattern is a named character's opening event chain that forks the timeline (Nepos). An EU5 mod can do the same at 395: Stilicho vs Rufinus/Eutropius, Alaric's choice, Gildo's revolt.

### Gaps
- There are no official statistics on which bookmark is most played. The popularity inference rests only on YouTube titles.
- I could not verify the exact featured-character list for each bookmark screen, because the wiki was blocked.

## 3. Flagship content: decisions, events, mechanics

### Takeaway
TFE's content is dense. There are dozens of *restore/form* decisions, both historical and alt-history (Rome, the dioceses, Parthia, Assyria, Carthage via a submod, and a whimsical "Pirate Empire of Illyria"). It has "struggles" for regional power contests, story-cycle event chains (Donatists, Attila, Third Temple), special buildings and wonders, migrations, and a Roman imperial government with a collapse mechanic, legions and competence.

### Cited Findings
Roman and imperial systems (Oct 2021 overhaul onward):
- An imperial government overhaul added "imperial laws, centralization/de-centralization laws, and imperial competence which includes the collapse mechanic". It also added an "Autocratic" government for former Roman provinces, a legion system that lets you create custom legions and "restore the praetorian guard", and decisions "Claim your emperor's throne", "Re-unite Rome", "Form the Red Sea empire" and "Overthrow the Emperor". — [Change notes p5](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=5)
- Jan 2022: a dynamic spawn for Islam and "all major events till the Islamic schism". New Caliphate, Islamic and Gupta governments, including a "Gupta Centralization" law. — [Change notes p5](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=5)
- Reign of the Apostate (Mar 2023) added "Create the Province of Germania" and "Restore Roman Dioceses" for Roman empires, with tooltips for restoring the Western dioceses. It also added "Hegemony Over Hispania" for the Visigoths, reworked the Hibernia/Caledonia decisions and fixed "Restore Hispania" and "Restore Septem". When "restore Roman Empire" is taken, Imperial elective succession is applied to the new title. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- House of Sassan (Jan 2023) added "a decision for bureaucratic empires to reform the ERE and WRE". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- The 1.9 patch added a decision for Roman emperors "to recruit regional commanders as well as retinue", and gave the ERE the Roman court and Late Roman units. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- Non-Romans can restore Rome: Frankish, Iberian and Brythonic rulers can restore it from Francia, Hispania or Britannia after establishing and fully controlling their starting empire. — [search summary of Workshop discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/2270547030/3269059787424254375) (snippet only, not fully verified)

Struggles and great-power mechanics (House of Sassan, Jan 2023):
- The "Romano-Ērānian Wars" struggle has four endings: Roman Dominance, Ērānian Dominance, Regional Independence, and "Supplant the Romans and Ērānian". A "Struggle for Greater Khorasan" ends in Dominance, Decentralized Politics or Silk Road Prosperity. There is also a new proxy-war mechanic, a Great Houses mechanic for Sassanid politics, the "Ascend the House" interaction, and a Mahestan elective. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- A "Britannic Struggle" (and an Iberian Struggle) also exist. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)

Restore/form decisions (a sample showing the range):
- Restore Parthia, Restore the Kingdom of Bosporus, Restore Assyria, Create the Caucasian Empire, "Establish a True Kingdom for the Suebians", "Liberate the Romans of the South" (Soissons), "Avenge the Kingdom of Worms" (Burgundy), "Avenge the Battle of Carrhae" (emperor), Reclaim Tisfun, "Urbanize the Silk Road", and "Construct the City of Baghdad" (Muslims). — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- "Heir of Attila", a decision and event chain for reunifying the Black Huns. The Attila event chain was also reworked. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- "The Pirate Empire of Illyria", added "because why not lol", an explicitly fun, non-historical formable. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)

Monument, building and "great work" decisions:
- Restore/Rebuild the Ma'rib Dam (with a special building), "Build the Great Gorgan Wall" (with a building), Construct the Khawarnaq Palace (with a Sinnimar event), Taq-Kasra at Tisfun, the Fortress of Oblivion, Petra, Diocletian's palace at Split, a Zoroastrian centre at Yazd, and an Aksum monument decision that gives a capital modifier. There is also a decision to rebuild the Third Temple, whose event chain "must be completed within one ruler's lifetime". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)

Religious and social event chains:
- A Donatist revolt story cycle in Africa (Donatist titles revert on winning), a Mazdakism event, "Expel Yemenite Christians", Behafaridism and Khurramism appearing only after Islam, and a "Perform Illyrian Sacrifice" decision (Cult of the Sun / Cult of the Serpent). — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- Culture flavour: Gothic culture-conversion decisions, a "Proclaim Law" decision for Germanics, "Instate the Dehqan Class" for the Shah, and building djeddars as Christian Berbers. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)

Migrations:
- Migration armies with spawned stacks. Oct 2021 "increased migration rates". Later notes rebalance migration armies, make some spawn armies non-inheritable, and make "AI Hun invader stacks non inheritable". A migration-strength game rule was added in 1.9. — [Change notes p5](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=5); [p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)

Loading screens and quotes:
- House of Sassan added "loading quotes". A separate vanilla-compatible "Historical Quotes" mod exists. — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3); [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)

Submod extension of the "restore" pillar:
- The Flavor Pack Submod (Askil) adds "Restore the Carthaginian Empire", "Revive Ancient Egyptian Empire", "Visit Laconia" (convert to Spartan culture) and an Urartian religion. — [Workshop 2887285681](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2887285681)

### Inferences
- The recipe is: every region gets at least one *restoration fantasy* decision tied to a real past polity, plus one *monument/great work* decision with a building payoff. Several regions also get a *multi-ending struggle*. This maps closely onto EU5 formable decisions, situations, monuments and disasters.
- Alt-history is sanctioned but framed. Endings like "Supplant the Romans and Ērānian", "Pirate Empire of Illyria" and non-Romans restoring Rome show TFE deliberately rewards divergence.
- Collapse is a *system* (imperial competence plus a collapse mechanic, which players can switch off), not a scripted event. That lets the player "save Rome".

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the formables for Francia, Hispania and Italia, and the exact "restore Rome" requirements, from the wiki's Decisions page (blocked). The evidence is indirect: change-note fixes and a search snippet.
- I found no evidence that TFE has its own achievements.
- The Sack of Rome 410 event content is mentioned only in the description. I found no detailed breakdown.

## 4. Presentation: what makes it feel Late Antique

### Takeaway
TFE's identity is strongly visual and aural. It uses a black-and-gold UI with Late Roman art motifs, a parchment/Latin "paper map" (widely compared to Total War: Attila), a complete original soundtrack by George Hammond, and custom 3D clothing, armour, headgear, artifacts and urban sprawl. Latinised character names (Iulianus, Iustinianus, Iulius Nepos, Flavius Stilicho) and a custom map font finish the effect. Some players think the Attila look is overdone.

### Cited Findings
- The UI replaces CK3's "vanilla, Gothic-styled interface with one that predominantly follows a black-gold color scheme and incorporates elements of Late Roman art." — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- "Soundtrack: The Fallen Eagle features a complete soundtrack composed by George Hammond." Hammond is also listed as a lead developer. — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- "Several new 3D assets for clothing, armor, headgear, artifacts, and on-map urban sprawl", credited to ツALU, Atreides, Celticus, Constantinus and Nessel. — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- Paper map: released standalone for vanilla as "TFE – Paper Map", which is Ironman and achievement compatible. It has 612+ collections and was still updated in Jul 2026. A user comment: "I love how it draws inspiration from Total War Attila while still fitting seamlessly into vanilla ... especially since Latin remained the lingua franca". Another: "all the other paper maps are UGLY!" — [Workshop 2449634219](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2449634219)
- Asset work in patches: "Added a new font for the map", "Revamped event icons", "Changed decision art", "Revamped the trading resource GFX assets to better match the vanilla style", new Persian/Sassanid armour sets, and many regional artifacts, e.g. "Silk Chlamys with Gold Tablion", "Scipio Eberneus", "Silver Pilos". — [Change notes p3](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/2243307127?p=3)
- Custom HUD tabs, e.g. a Trade tab. A user noted other mods reverting "the tabs on the right of the screen ... to their vanilla look, which means removing the new tabs added by TFE such as the trade tab." — [Extended Collection comments](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/comments/2929347724)
- Character naming uses Latin forms: Iulianus, Iustinianus, Iulius Nepos, Constantinus III. — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- YouTube review framing: "TW: ATTILA IN CK3! | The Fallen Eagle Overhaul Mod". — [YouTube (HForHavoc)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VvVeicQOtUI)
- A dissenting view (comment on a rival mod's page): "The Fallen eagle should drop the Total War Attila aesthetics though in my opinion". — [WtWSMS comments](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2858562094)

### Inferences
- TFE's cheapest high-impact wins are the black/gold Late Roman UI skin, the parchment Latin map and Latinised names. The paper map alone was popular enough to ship as a vanilla mod. For EU5, a map-mode or lens restyle plus Latin localisation of place and character names would give most of the "feel" without new 3D assets.
- Loading-screen quotes are an easy borrow.

### Gaps
- I found no details on the coat-of-arms approach or on portrait and clothing specifics beyond "clothing, armor, headgear". Details of the loading-screen art were not found.

## 5. Development history, team, submods and comparables

### Takeaway
TFE is **CK3-native**. It was first published on the Workshop on Sep 29, 2020, weeks after CK3 launched, by AncientConqueror. I found no evidence of a CK2 predecessor under the TFE name; the CK2-lineage Late Antiquity mod is *When the World Stopped Making Sense* (WtWSMS). TFE has grown into a large team (7 leads and about 30 developers) and a big ecosystem: roughly 160k subscribers, 84 dependent items and 7,000+ collections. It ships themed named updates.

## 6. Reception: praise and criticism

### Takeaway
TFE is the most-subscribed Late Antiquity mod for CK3 (about 160k subscribers, 6.1k ratings). Players praise its immersion, its aesthetics (above all the paper map), its breadth of flavour, and the "save Rome" fantasy. Criticism clusters around **compatibility fragility** (it depends on its own county and duchy layout and custom HUD tabs), **long update gaps after CK3 patches** (users repeatedly asking for updates), **crashes**, and some **migration/army-spawn balance** issues, which the devs have kept tuning. Direct Reddit sentiment and AI-collapse-pace complaints could not be checked.

### Cited Findings
- Scale: 6,117 ratings and 159,828 subscribers. — [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2243307127)
- Praise:
  - Submod author: "among all the mods I've tried, none have brought me as much enjoyment as The Fallen Eagle". — [Flavor Pack](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=2887285681)

### Inferences
- The Workshop star rating is not in the fetched text. With 6.1k ratings and about 160k active subscribers after six years, reception is clearly strongly positive overall.
- Lessons for EU5: (1) keep scripted content robust to map edits, or keep the map layer owned by the mod; (2) expose balance knobs such as collapse strength and migration strength as game rules; (3) turn spawned migration armies into a player-visible, tunable system rather than flooding stacks; (4) a big-bang not-save-compatible update is accepted if announced.
